# Netherlands employer recognition of CAISP, CAASE and HTB COAE

Retrieval date: 2026-11-20. Scope: public first-party postings only (employer careers pages or the employer's own ATS). Convenience sample, not representative.

## Headline findings

1. **Only one of the three credentials is named in the sample: HTB COAE.** It appears in one posting, Bureau Veritas Cybersecurity (formerly Secura), "Senior AI Security Penetration Tester". It is listed as an example certification in a "You have" list (see caveat below).
2. **CAISP and CAASE are not named in any of the 10 postings read.** Practical DevSecOps is not mentioned either.
3. There is **insufficient direct employer evidence to rank the three credentials**. One mention of one credential in one posting is not a ranking. Absence of a mention does not mean these credentials are unrecognised.
4. Where certifications appear in offensive roles, they are mostly OSCP, OSEP, OSWE, CRTO and similar. Architecture roles name TOGAF, CISSP, CISM, CCSK and cloud vendor certificates. These are context only.
5. The skills employers ask for are more consistent than the certifications. They include LLM and generative-AI testing (prompt injection, jailbreaks), AI agents, red teaming (TIBER/ART), cloud and API security, and threat-led architecture assessment.

## Method

- Employers touched: 9 (cap was 12). They are Bureau Veritas Cybersecurity, Northwave, Deloitte NL, ABN AMRO, NCC Group NL, Adyen, ING, Booking.com and Rabobank.
- Postings were discovered through each employer's own careers page, sitemap or official ATS feed (Greenhouse, Recruitee, Pinpoint). Each was then read directly.
- "Status" means the posting was listed on the employer site on the retrieval date. It is not proof the vacancy is open. `datePosted` comes from the page's structured data.
- Quotes are verbatim. Deloitte quotes are in Dutch with my translation in brackets.

## What the postings show (skills only, no demand statistics)

Employers in this sample ask for:
- testing LLMs and generative AI: prompt injection, jailbreaks, data leakage, model manipulation, the OWASP LLM Top 10, LLM red teaming, adversarial ML (Bureau Veritas);
- agentic AI: AI agents and autonomous workflows as a test target (Bureau Veritas), and agentic AI as a tool for red teams (Deloitte);
- red teaming: TIBER, ART and CBEST (Northwave, Deloitte);
- cloud, API and CI/CD security, and Kubernetes (Bureau Veritas, Deloitte);
- threat-led architecture assessment and secure design (Deloitte Security Domain Architect);
- AI-assisted security testing (ABN AMRO).

No AI-security architecture posting was found, so the architecture track is covered only by one adjacent, non-AI role (Deloitte, #8).

## Limitations

- Sample of 9 employers and 10 postings, chosen for reachability. It is not representative and supports no demand statistics.
- No mention, a blocked page or a scan hit of zero does not show that an employer does not recognise a credential. Keyword scans covered only the visible page text, so a certification in a non-rendered section could be missed.
- COAE is named in one posting. Whether it is required or preferred is not determinable from the page text. A single mention does not rank COAE above CAISP or CAASE.
- Deloitte Dutch quotes were translated by me.
- Listing status was inferred from presence on the employer site on 2026-11-20. Closure was not verified.
- Adyen was scanned for Amsterdam postings only, using the keyword and title filters in the repeat steps below.

## How to repeat

1. Bureau Veritas Cybersecurity: open https://cybersecurity.bureauveritas.com/careers (https://www.secura.com/careers redirects there) and read the Technology section.
2. Northwave: open https://careers.northwave-cybersecurity.com/ (Recruitee).
3. Deloitte NL: read https://www.werkenbijdeloitte.nl/sitemap.xml, filter `/vacature/` URLs for cyber, security, red-team, offensive, architect and ai, then open each.
4. ABN AMRO: read https://www.werkenbijabnamro.nl/sitemap.vacancy.xml and filter the same way.
5. Adyen: query `https://boards-api.greenhouse.io/v1/boards/adyen/jobs?content=true`, keep Amsterdam postings, and search the content for certification terms.
6. NCC Group: `https://nccgroup.pinpointhq.com/postings.json`.
7. ING: `https://careers.ing.com/sitemap.xml`, then job URLs.
8. For each posting, search the text for: CAISP, CAASE, COAE, Practical DevSecOps, Hack The Box, "Certified AI Security", "Offensive AI", OSCP, CISSP, CCSP, prompt injection, LLM, agentic, red team, threat model.
9. Record datePosted from the page's JSON-LD.
