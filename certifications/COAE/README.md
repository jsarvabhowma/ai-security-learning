# HTB Certified Offensive AI Expert (COAE)

**Status:** Optional third step after [CAASE](../CAASE/README.md).

Official page: [HTB COAE](https://academy.hackthebox.com/preview/certifications/htb-certified-offensive-ai-expert)

## Why

Deepens offensive AI skills through a full engagement and a professional report.

## Exam (as published on 2026-11-20)

- Practical attacks against hosted AI systems; no multiple-choice questions.
- Seven days of lab access, with the report due within seven days of starting.
- The report must propose remediation and meet client-ready standards.
- Prerequisite: complete the full [AI Red Teamer](https://academy.hackthebox.com/path/preview/ai-red-teamer)
  job-role path (12 modules).

## Official knowledge domains

- Machine learning and AI fundamentals
- Evasion and data attacks
- Prompt injection, jailbreaking and LLM output exploitation
- AI application and system security, including MCP vulnerabilities
- Model reverse engineering and denial of service
- AI defence, and privacy attacks and defences

## Decision before starting

- [ ] Confirm current voucher and AI Red Teamer path costs.
- [ ] Reserve time for the 7-day exam window.
