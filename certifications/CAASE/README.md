# Certified Agentic AI Security Expert (CAASE)

**Status:** Paid by employer; second step after [CAISP](../CAISP/README.md).

Official page: [Practical DevSecOps CAASE](https://www.practical-devsecops.com/certified-agentic-ai-security-expert/)

## Why

Extends CAISP to the agentic layer: attacking and defending AI agents rather
than the underlying model.

## Course focus (as published on 2026-11-20)

Offence-and-defence training for agents that plan, remember, call tools, browse
the web, write and execute code, and delegate to other agents.

## Exam (as published on 2026-11-20)

- Practical, not multiple choice: five challenges in six hours.
- A further 24 hours to submit a written report.
- At least 80 out of 100 points required to pass; one attempt included.
