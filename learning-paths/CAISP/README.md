# Certified AI Security Professional (CAISP)

**Status:** Paid by employer; first certification, after the [Hugging Face Agents Course](../hugging-face-agents/README.md) foundation step.

Official page: [Practical DevSecOps CAISP](https://www.practical-devsecops.com/certified-ai-security-professional/)

## Why

Builds a practical foundation in AI and LLM security before moving to agents.
The Hugging Face Agents Course Units 1–2 are the recommended preparation for
agent concepts, not a CAISP prerequisite.

## Course focus (as published on 2026-11-20)

- OWASP Top 10 vulnerabilities for LLM applications
- Model attacks
- AI supply chain risks
- AI threat modelling
- MITRE ATLAS defences

## Exam (as published on 2026-11-20)

- Online, task-based and not multiple choice: five challenges in six hours.
- A further 24 hours to submit a written report.
- At least 80 points required to pass.
- See the [official exam page](https://www.practical-devsecops.com/exam-and-certification/)
  for rules, including tools that are not allowed.
